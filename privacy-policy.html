Your Information & Our Use

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how booking inquiry information is collected, used, and handled through this static website.

Last updated: July 27, 2026

1. Information you provide

When you use a contact or booking form, call, WhatsApp, or email us, you may provide your name, phone number, email address, event date, city, venue, event type, guest count, preferred band format, and other event details.

2. How information is used

Information is used to respond to inquiries, discuss availability, recommend a suitable performance format, coordinate event requirements, prepare booking terms, and communicate about the requested service.

3. Form processing

This static website uses FormSubmit to route form submissions to armybandsherpunjab@gmail.com. Form submission therefore involves a third-party form delivery service. If you prefer not to use the web form, contact the booking team directly by phone, WhatsApp, or email.

4. Sharing

Inquiry details are not sold. Information may be shared only where reasonably necessary to respond to the inquiry, coordinate the requested event, comply with law, or protect the safety and rights of the parties involved.

5. Retention

Booking and inquiry information may be kept for as long as reasonably needed for communication, event coordination, record keeping, dispute resolution, or legal obligations.

6. Cookies and analytics

This version of the website does not include advertising cookies or a dedicated analytics script. Third-party resources such as Bootstrap CDN and the form delivery service may process technical request data according to their own policies.

7. Your choices

You may ask to correct or delete inquiry information held by the booking team, subject to legitimate record-keeping or legal requirements. Contact us using the details below.

8. Security

Reasonable care is taken with inquiry information, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Children

The website is intended for event inquiries by adults and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, email armybandsherpunjab@gmail.com or call +91 94631 35783.
