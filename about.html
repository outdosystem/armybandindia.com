About Us

Meet the ceremonial ensemble behind The Royal Bagpipe Band Shere Punjab and the approach that shapes every performance.

18Artist Ensemble
Our Identity

More than live music—a complete ceremonial presentation.

The Royal Bagpipe Band Shere Punjab combines musicianship, formation, uniform, movement, and timing to give milestone events a distinctive sense of occasion.

Our work spans wedding entrances, processions, corporate welcomes, VIP arrivals, campus ceremonies, community programs, and national celebrations. The format changes, but the standard remains the same: prepared musicians, disciplined visual order, clear coordination, and respect for the event.

Every booking begins with practical questions about the venue, performance route, key timing, audience, and desired atmosphere. This allows the band experience to feel integrated into the event instead of added at the last minute.

  • Ceremonial bagpipes, brass, and drums
  • White and navy formal uniform options
  • Procession, arrival, welcome, and stage formats
  • Event planning around route and timing
Our Standards

The values behind every formation.

A premium event experience depends on the details guests may not consciously notice—but will always feel.

Discipline

Prepared arrival, coordinated movement, and a composed presentation from reporting to completion.

Musicianship

Bagpipes, brass, and percussion brought together to serve the ceremony and the space.

Coordination

Clear communication around route, entry cue, venue access, and event team requirements.

Presentation

Uniform detail, spacing, formation, and sightlines designed for visual impact.

The Full Ensemble

Built to scale from a focused entrance to a grand formation.

The supplied brand artwork presents a full 18-artist ensemble. Smaller or alternate lineups can be discussed according to venue size, performance route, event format, and travel requirements.
